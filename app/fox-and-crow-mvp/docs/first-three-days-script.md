# 《狐狸与乌鸦：循环之日》前三天剧本（第三稿）

## 一、叙事目的

前三天不是把同一则寓言机械播放三遍，而是让狐狸对循环的认识逐日推进：

1. 第一天：狐狸只想得到奶酪，完整经历原版寓言。
2. 第二天：狐狸发现昨天的成果全部复原，带着惊讶与怀疑重演旧计，以此验证乌鸦是否记得昨天。
3. 第三天：狐狸已经确认乌鸦与奶酪会重复，第一次离开原版寓言的行动路线，探索周围环境并测试整个世界是否也会复原。

第一、二天为固定序章。第三天开始同时提供作者设计的行动路线和 SAY / DO 自由行动。狐狸保留记忆；乌鸦是否保留记忆，在前三天内不作权威结论，只通过她看似毫无认出的反应制造疑问。

Day 10 之前，作者路线和系统默认叙述不主动提示乌鸦正在寻找什么，也不提及她的异常视线、紧张声音或任何特定搜索地点。

## 二、第一天：完成寓言

**当天功能：** 第一天沿用现有故事，不改变情节。

### 1. The First Morning

You wake at the edge of a forest clearing. 

Morning light filters through the leaves. High on a branch sits a crow with a piece of cheese in her beak.

**Choice:** Watch the crow in the tree.

### 2. Under the Tree

You step beneath the branch and look up. The cheese is held tight in the crow's closed beak, its pale edge catching the morning light.

If you can make her open her beak, the cheese will fall.

**Choice:** Begin by praising her feathers.

### 3. Beautiful Feathers

“Good morning, Miss Crow,” you say with your most sincere smile. “What beautiful feathers you have. No one else in this forest looks half as splendid.”

The crow hears your praise and lifts her chest proudly. She still holds the cheese tight, but now she cannot look away.

**Choice:** Ask her to sing for you.

### 4. The Finest Voice in the Forest

“A crow so beautiful must also have the finest voice in the forest,” you say. “Would you sing a song and let me hear it?”

The crow raises her head. She can hardly wait to prove that your praise is true.

**Choice:** Wait for her to open her beak.

### 5. The Cheese

“Caw—”

The moment the crow opens her beak, the cheese tumbles from the branch. You step forward and catch it neatly.

The trick has worked. At last, you have something to eat.

**Choice:** Eat the cheese and face the evening.

### 6. The Day Ends

You have the cheese. By every rule you know, that should mean you have won.

But as the sun sinks behind the trees, the colors drain from the clearing. Shadows slide back to their old places. The wind moves backward through the leaves, and the fullness in your stomach disappears.

Somewhere above, you hear the beat of a crow's wings.

**Choice:** Sleep till the next day.

## 三、第二天：验证异常

**当天功能：** 让狐狸明确意识到“奶酪重新出现”和“乌鸦似乎忘记被骗”这两个异常。他重复骗术，不是因为毫无变化，而是想验证昨天是否真实、乌鸦是否记得。

### 1. The Morning Again

You wake with the taste of cheese still lingering in your mouth.

For a moment, you expect to see an empty branch. Instead, the crow is perched above the clearing with a piece of cheese in her beak—just as she was yesterday.

Another piece? So soon? 

You stare up at her. She gives no sign that she recognizes the fox who tricked her only yesterday. Has she truly forgotten?

**Choice:** Go closer and see whether she remembers you.

### 2. Under the Same Tree

You step beneath the branch. The crow watches you without alarm. There is no anger in her eyes, no embarrassment, not even the caution of someone who has already lost one meal to you.

The cheese is held tight in her closed beak. The same simple plan presents itself again: make her open it, and the cheese will fall.

You decide to repeat yesterday's words exactly. If she remembers, she cannot possibly fall for them twice.

**Choice:** Test her with the same compliment.

### 3. The Same Beautiful Feathers

“Good morning, Miss Crow,” you say. “What beautiful feathers you have. No one else in this forest looks half as splendid.”

The crow lifts her chest proudly.

It is not merely a similar reaction. The tilt of her head, the small ruffle along her neck, even the pleased gleam in her eye arrive exactly when you remember them.

She does not remember. Or the morning will not allow her to.

**Choice:** Ask for the same song.

### 4. The Same Finest Voice

“A crow so beautiful must also have the finest voice in the forest,” you continue. “Would you sing a song and let me hear it?”

Part of you expects her to clamp her beak shut and expose the joke.

Instead, she raises her head, eager to sing—exactly as before.

**Choice:** Wait and see whether the cheese falls again.

### 5. The Cheese, Again

“Caw—”

The cheese falls. Your body moves before surprise can slow it, and you catch the piece neatly between your paws.

The trick has worked a second time. Yet the cheese feels less like a prize than a piece of evidence.

Yesterday happened. The world put everything back. Whether the crow forgot—or was made to forget—you still cannot tell.

**Choice:** Eat the cheese and watch what the evening does.

### 6. The Second Evening

This time you do not mistake success for an ending.

You watch the last light leave the clearing. The taste fades from your mouth. The weight in your stomach vanishes. Leaves lift from the ground and return to their branches.

Just before the darkness closes, you look up at the crow and wonder whether tomorrow she will forget you again.

**Choice:** Wake up again.

## 四、第三天：新增“探索世界”脚本

**当天功能：** 第三天新增第二套完整的作者脚本。玩家可以选择要进入哪套脚本；进入“探索世界”后，后续作者选项全部围绕探索、记录和实验展开，不再返回欺骗乌鸦的流程。SAY / DO 自由行动始终作为第三种入口保留。

### 1. The Third Morning：选择脚本

Before you open your eyes, you know where the light will fall, how the leaves will stir, and which branch will creak above you.

The crow is there. The cheese is there. Neither surprises you now.

For two mornings, that branch has been the whole world: the hunger below, the food above, and the trick between them. But the clearing continues beyond the tree.

If the day can restore the cheese, what else does it restore?

**Script 1:** Repeat the familiar story.

- 进入原有的“奉承乌鸦 → 骗取奶酪”作者脚本。
- 该脚本继续使用第一、二天已有的 Passage，不属于本节新增内容。

**Script 2:** Explore the world beyond the tree.

- 进入下面新增的“探索世界”作者脚本。
- 进入后不再出现奉承、唱歌或等待奶酪掉落等选项。

**Free action:** SAY / DO something else.

- 不进入任何预设脚本，由玩家自行决定行动。

### 2. Explore the Clearing：选择探索方向

You turn away from the branch before the familiar compliment can form.

Beyond the clearing, a narrow path passes between two pines. A shallow stream catches the morning light farther downhill. On the opposite side, the grass rises toward a line of dark rocks.

These places were always here. Hunger simply made them irrelevant.

**Choice A:** Follow the path between the pines.

**Choice B:** Walk downstream and study the water.

**Choice C:** Circle the edge of the clearing.

三项都属于“探索世界”脚本，并分别进入一个短探索分支。

### 3A. The Pine Path

You follow the narrow path until the old tree disappears behind you.

A hollow stump stands where the path bends. Beside it, yellow flowers grow through a split in the earth. You count your steps and repeat each landmark silently: two pines, the fallen log, the hollow stump, the yellow flowers.

For the first time, the day feels larger than the fable told about it.

**Choice:** Test whether this path remembers your visit.

### 3B. Downstream

You follow the stream away from the clearing.

The water passes over twelve pale stones before slipping beneath a fallen trunk. You watch a leaf turn twice in the same small whirlpool, then continue downstream.

You memorize the stones, the trunk, and the sound of the current. Tomorrow, you will know whether the stream repeats as precisely as the tree.

**Choice:** Change something beside the stream.

### 3C. The Edge of the Clearing

You walk the full boundary of the clearing instead of approaching its center.

On one side, thick ferns conceal the ground. On the other, three dark rocks form a crooked line. Farther around, an animal trail disappears beneath low branches.

You build a map in your memory: tree, rocks, trail, ferns, stream, and back to the tree.

**Choice:** Leave a marker at the clearing's edge.

### 4. A Mark for Tomorrow：选择实验

三个探索分支在这里汇合。玩家根据自己走过的路线选择一种验证循环的实验：

**Choice A:** Move a smooth white stone into the hollow stump.

**Choice B:** Scratch a small cross into the damp earth.

**Choice C:** Stack three fallen twigs against one of the dark rocks.

选择后显示共同结果：

The change is simple enough to recognize and small enough that no wind or animal should undo it before morning.

You repeat the location and the shape of your marker until you are certain you will remember both.

### 5. How Far Does the Day Go?：继续探索

The light has begun to change, but there is still time to test one more part of the world.

**Choice A:** Go farther and see how far the path continues.

**Choice B:** Return to the marker and make sure it remains in place.

**Choice C:** Find a high point and memorize the shape of the land.

无论选择哪一项，本日的收获都是一张更完整的环境地图和一个等待次日验证的标记，而不是关于乌鸦的新线索。

### 6. The Third Evening

The clearing begins to rewind for the third time. Shadows retreat. Bent grass rises beneath your footprints. The routes you walked disappear into darkness.

Somewhere beyond the trees, the marker you left is returned to its original state.

Tomorrow, you can retrace your route and see whether the world restored it exactly.

The old story is still waiting, but it is no longer the only script you know.

**Choice:** Carry your map of the world into another morning.

## 五、第四天自由行动的承接句

前三天剧本结束后，进入当前的 Story-first 自由行动界面：

> I wake beneath the same tree, with the same crow above me and morning arranged exactly where it was before. The old choices are still waiting, but they are no longer the only ones I know.
>
> I remember the route I chose beyond the clearing and the marker I left before the world rewound. I can retrace my steps, test another part of the world, return to the old script, speak, wait, or try something the written story never anticipated.

这一承接只告诉玩家“可以打破旧剧本”和“环境会回应行动”，不提示乌鸦另有目标，也不规定玩家必须调查任何特定地点。

## 六、内容验收条件

- 第一天保持原版寓言功能，不提前暴露循环知识。
- 第二天开场明确出现狐狸对奶酪再次出现的惊讶，以及“乌鸦是否忘了昨天被骗”的疑问。
- 第二天每次重复旧台词都有“验证乌鸦记忆”的动机，不呈现为无意识复读。
- 第三天先提供“重复熟悉故事”“进入探索世界脚本”“SAY / DO 自由行动”三个入口。
- 玩家进入探索世界脚本后，所有预设选项只围绕探索方向、环境地标、循环实验和继续深入展开。
- 探索脚本通过路径、地标和人为标记扩展世界，不提示乌鸦正在寻找什么。
- Day 4 的默认承接不包含乌鸦的异常视线、声音、灌木丛或项链线索。
- Day 10 之前，系统不主动呈现“乌鸦正在寻找什么”的作者提示。
- 三天的狐狸认知依次为：不知道循环、验证循环、测试循环世界的范围。
